# Privacy Policy for LingvoMate

**Effective Date:** August 26, 2026

LingvoMate ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose your personal information when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

- **Account Information:** When you create an account, we collect your email address, profile name, and selected avatar via **Firebase Authentication**.
- **Usage Data & Progress:** Your learning progress, vocabulary status, and app settings are stored using **Firebase Firestore** and local storage to synchronize your experience.
- **Device Information:** Basic device information for app functionality and notification delivery (if enabled).

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the LingvoMate application.
- Synchronize your vocabulary learning progress across devices.
- Send daily study reminders (if enabled in settings).
- Allow account recovery and security verification.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely processed and stored using **Google Firebase** services. We implement security best practices to protect your data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

---

### 4. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You have full control over your personal data:
- **Delete Account:** You can permanently delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the app by going to **Settings → Delete Account**.
- **Data Removal:** Deleting your account instantly removes your authentication record and Firestore database entries.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with third-party service providers that have their own privacy policies:
- **Firebase Authentication & Firestore:** [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

LingvoMate does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
