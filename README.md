# Privacy Policy for LingvoMate

**Effective Date: August 28, 2026**

LingvoMate ("LingvoMate", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and what choices you have when using the LingvoMate mobile application.

By using LingvoMate, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create or use a LingvoMate account, we may collect information such as:

- Email address
- Profile or display name
- Selected avatar or profile settings
- Authentication-related information

Authentication is provided through Google Firebase Authentication.

### Learning Progress and App Data

To provide and synchronize your learning experience, we may store information such as:

- Learned words and vocabulary progress
- Lesson progress
- Learning activity
- App preferences and settings
- Reminder preferences

Some information is stored locally on your device, while information required for account synchronization may be stored using Firebase Cloud Firestore.

### Device and Technical Information

Our service providers may automatically process certain technical information, including:

- IP address and approximate/general location derived from IP address
- Device or advertising identifiers
- App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views
- Diagnostic and performance information
- App and device configuration information

This information may be processed for application functionality, advertising, analytics, security, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- Create and manage your LingvoMate account
- Provide lessons and vocabulary-learning features
- Synchronize learning progress across devices
- Save your preferences and app settings
- Provide optional study reminders
- Provide rewarded advertising
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the application
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

---

## 3. Advertising

LingvoMate uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

LingvoMate may offer optional rewarded advertisements. For example, you may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for access to certain app functionality or lessons.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

- IP address
- Device and advertising identifiers
- App interactions
- Advertisement interactions and video views
- Diagnostic information
- Approximate location derived from IP address

Advertising data may be used to provide ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and perform analytics.

Where required by applicable law, including for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, LingvoMate uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent.

When available or legally required, users may review or change their advertising privacy choices from the app's privacy settings.

---

## 4. Firebase Services

LingvoMate uses Google Firebase services, including:

- Firebase Authentication — for account authentication and account management
- Cloud Firestore — for storing and synchronizing account and learning data
- Firebase Remote Config — for remotely configuring certain application behavior

Google may process technical and service-related information when providing these services.

---

## 5. Notifications

LingvoMate may send optional study reminders if you enable notifications.

You can enable or disable notification permissions through your device settings. Reminder preferences may also be managed through LingvoMate.

---

## 6. Data Storage and International Processing

LingvoMate uses services provided by Google and Firebase.

Information may be processed or stored on servers located outside your country of residence, depending on the service being used and the infrastructure operated by our service providers.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

---

## 7. Data Retention

We retain account and learning information for as long as your account remains active or as reasonably necessary to provide LingvoMate.

When you delete your account, LingvoMate will request deletion of the account information and associated application data that we control.

Certain limited information may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, fraud-prevention systems, or service-provider systems where retention is required for technical, security, legal, or regulatory purposes.

---

## 8. Account Deletion and Your Choices

You can request permanent deletion of your LingvoMate account directly from the application:

**Settings → Delete Account**

Deleting your account removes access to the account and initiates deletion of associated account and learning data handled by LingvoMate.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

- Request access to your personal information
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address provided below.
